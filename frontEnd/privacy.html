Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

JoshTracker is a private, personal app used by two people. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

JoshTracker reads flight events from the owner's Google Calendar (read-only) to find upcoming flights. It does not read, store, or share any other calendar data.

What it stores

It stores only flight details (flight number, airports, times, and status) for current and upcoming flights. Flights are deleted about 24 hours after they land. It also stores the device addresses needed to send push alerts to the two signed-in users.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Flight numbers are sent to a flight-status service only to look up times and status.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner directly.
