How it works

Always know where I am

  • The headlineTells you where I am right now: home, on the road, at a hotel, or in the air.
  • The countdownCounts down to my next takeoff or landing, and shows any delays.
  • The mapShows my plane moving across the country when I fly.
  • Home inCounts down to the minute I'm back. On driving trips, it counts down to hotel checkout.
  • This weekWhere I'll be each day: flights, hotel nights, and home. Tap one for details. Plus a countdown to our next date.

It updates by itself. My flights and hotel stays come straight from my calendar, so you never have to ask.

Alerts

  • When I land, and when I'm home
